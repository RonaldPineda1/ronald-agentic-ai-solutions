The process for tracking your package typically involves the following steps:

1. Check the shipping carrier's website: Most shipping carriers, such as UPS, FedEx, and USPS, allow you to track your package online. Simply visit their website, enter your tracking number (usually found on the package or in an email confirmation), and click "Track" to see the status of your package.
2. Use a package tracking app: There are several third-party apps available that can help you track your package, such as ShipStation, TrackR, or Package Guard. These apps often allow you to enter your tracking number and receive updates on the status of your package.
3. Contact the shipping carrier directly: If you're having trouble finding your tracking information online, you can contact the shipping carrier's customer service department directly. They may be able to provide you with the current status of your package or help you resolve any issues that arise during transit.

By following these steps, you should be able to keep track of your package and stay informed about its location and delivery status.,
